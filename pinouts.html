📌 Spark the Circuit - Board Pinouts Reference Guide

Comprehensive pin mappings, power supply rails, communication bus layouts, and specialized peripheral assignments for 20 major development boards.

1. Arduino Uno R3 Pinout

  • Digital Pins 0-13: Digital I/O. Pins 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11 support PWM. Pins 0 (RX) and 1 (TX) handle Hardware Serial.
  • Analog Inputs A0-A5: 10-bit ADC resolution (can double as digital pins 14-19).
  • Power Rails: 5V regulated output, 3.3V output (~50mA), VIN barrel input, GND.
  • Communication: SPI (Pins 10, 11, 12, 13), I2C (A4 for SDA, A5 for SCL).

2. Arduino Uno R4 Minima Pinout

  • Digital Pins 0-13: 32-bit 5V tolerant I/O with enhanced sink/source current capacity.
  • PWM Channels: Expanded hardware PWM across multiple timer channels.
  • Analog Peripherals: 14-bit ADC resolution inputs plus 12-bit true Analog Output (DAC).
  • Busses: SPI, I2C, and dedicated CAN bus interface pins.

3. Arduino Mega 2560 Pinout

  • Digital I/O: 54 digital pins, with 15 providing PWM output capability.
  • Analog Channels: 16 dedicated high-precision analog input pins (A0-A15).
  • Serial Ports: 4 independent hardware UART serial ports (Serial0 to Serial3).
  • Communication: Dedicated SPI pins (50-53) and I2C pins (SDA on 20, SCL on 21).

4. Arduino Nano Pinout

  • Digital Pins D0-D13: Standard digital I/O with hardware serial on D0/D1 and PWM on D3, D5, D6, D9, D10, D11.
  • Analog Inputs: 8 analog channels (A0 to A7) in a compact DIP layout.
  • Power: 5V regulated output, 3.3V output, VIN input, dual GND pins.

5. ESP32 Dev Module Pinout

  • GPIO Pins: 36 digital multi-function pins supporting touch, PWM, ADC, and DAC.
  • Analog Inputs: 12-bit ADCs on ADC1 (GPIO 32-39) and ADC2. Note: ADC2 is disabled when Wi-Fi is active.
  • Communication: 3 UARTs, 3 SPI busses, 2 I2C interfaces.
  • Power: 3.3V logic levels (Strictly non-5V tolerant on GPIO). VIN 5V USB power rail.

6. ESP32-CAM Pinout

  • Camera Interface: Dedicated parallel camera port connected to OV2640 sensor.
  • MicroSD Card: SPI communication mapped to GPIO 14 (CLK), 15 (CMD), 2 (D0), 4 (D1), 12 (D2), 13 (D3).
  • Free GPIOs: GPIO 0, 3 (RX), 1 (TX), 16 (Flash LED). Note GPIO 0 must be pulled LOW for flashing.

7. Raspberry Pi Pico Pinout

  • GPIO Pins: 40 physical pins, with 26 multi-function 3.3V digital GPIOs.
  • Analog Channels: 3 ADC inputs (ADC0, ADC1, ADC2) plus internal temperature sensor channel.
  • Peripherals: 2 SPI controllers, 2 I2C controllers, 2 UART channels, 16 PWM channels, and Programmable I/O (PIO).

8. Raspberry Pi Pico W Pinout

  • Wireless Module: Infineon CYW43439 connected via SPI to the RP2040 chip.
  • GPIO Mapping: Identical physical pinout to standard Pico, with internal GPIO 23 controlling wireless power save.

9. NodeMCU ESP8266 Pinout

  • Digital Pins: D0 to D8 mapped to internal ESP8266 GPIOs (GPIO 16, 5, 4, 0, 2, 14, 12, 13, 15).
  • Analog Pin: Single dedicated analog input pin (A0) supporting 0-1V input range.
  • Power Rails: 3.3V output, 5V USB output pin, GND.

10. STM32F103C8T6 Blue Pill Pinout

  • Digital I/O: 37 5V-tolerant GPIO pins mapped across Port A, B, and C.
  • Analog Inputs: 10 12-bit ADC channels.
  • Serial Buses: 3 USARTs, 2 SPI interfaces, 2 I2C channels, and USB 2.0 full-speed interface.

11. STM32F411 Black Pill Pinout

  • GPIOs: High-speed 3.3V ARM Cortex-M4 GPIOs with flexible alternate function remapping.
  • Storage: SDIO interface for high-speed micro SD card communication.
  • Peripherals: 5 SPI, 3 I2C, 3 USART, and 2 I2S audio interfaces.

12. Teensy 4.0 Pinout

  • Digital I/O: 40 digital pin locations, 34 of which are 3.3V tolerant breadboard friendly.
  • Analog Inputs: 2 dedicated analog inputs sampled by dual high-speed ADCs.
  • Specialized: 3 I2C, 3 SPI, 7 Serial UARTs, and dedicated digital audio I2S pins.

13. Teensy 4.1 Pinout

  • Expanded I/O: 55 total accessible pin locations, including underside solder pads for memory and Ethernet PHY.
  • Storage: Onboard MicroSD socket wired directly to SDHC controller lines.

14. Seeeduino XIAO Pinout

  • Digital Pins: 11 digital pins supporting PWM on all channels.
  • Analog Inputs: 11 analog pins (10-bit ADC).
  • Interfaces: 1 UART, 1 I2C, 1 SPI, and 1 DAC output channel in a miniature DIP package.

15. Arduino Leonardo Pinout

  • Native USB: ATmega32U4 handling hardware USB HID without secondary bridge chips.
  • Digital I/O: 20 pins, 7 PWM channels, 12 analog inputs. Separate hardware serial on pins 0 & 1.

16. Arduino Due Pinout

  • 3.3V Logic: 54 digital pins (12 PWM), 12 analog inputs, 4 UARTs, 2 DAC outputs, and 2 I2C lines. Strictly 3.3V non-5V tolerant.

17. Wemos D1 Mini Pinout

  • Compact ESP8266: D1 (SCL), D2 (SDA), D3 (GPIO0), D4 (GPIO2 / LED), D5 (SCK), D6 (MISO), D7 (MOSI), D8 (CS), TX, RX, A0.

18. Digispark Attiny85 Pinout

  • Micro Pinout: P0 (PWM/I2C), P1 (PWM/LED), P2 (Analog/I2C), P3 (USB D+), P4 (USB D-/Analog), P5 (Reset).

19. Adafruit Feather M0 Pinout

  • Feather Standard: ATSAMD21G18 ARM processor with dedicated LiPo battery charging pins (BAT, EN), 20 GPIOs, and SPI/I2C headers.

20. BBC micro:bit v2 Pinout

  • Edge Connector: Large ring pins 0, 1, 2 for alligator clips plus 3V, GND, and comprehensive digital/analog bus access along the bottom edge.
