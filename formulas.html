📐 15 Essential Engineering Formulas

Core electrical and electronics equations for hardware design and calculations.

1. Ohm's Law (Voltage)

Calculate voltage given current and resistance.

V = I × R

2. Ohm's Law (Current)

Calculate current given voltage and resistance.

I = V / R

3. Ohm's Law (Resistance)

Calculate resistance given voltage and current.

R = V / I

4. Electrical Power

Calculate total power dissipation from voltage and current.

P = V × I

5. Power via Resistance

Power calculation using current squared and resistance.

P = I² × R

6. LED Current-Limiting Resistor

Calculate series resistor required for safe LED operation.

R = (Vs - Vf) / If

7. Voltage Divider

Calculate output voltage from two series resistors.

Vout = Vin × (R2 / (R1 + R2))

8. Capacitive Reactance

Opposition to AC current flow offered by a capacitor.

Xc = 1 / (2 × π × f × C)

9. Inductive Reactance

Opposition to AC current flow offered by an inductor.

Xl = 2 × π × f × L

10. RC Low-Pass Filter Cutoff Frequency

-3dB cutoff frequency for an RC filter circuit.

fc = 1 / (2 × π × R × C)

11. Resistors in Series

Total equivalent resistance for series connected resistors.

R_total = R1 + R2 + ... + Rn

12. Resistors in Parallel

Total equivalent resistance for parallel connected resistors.

1 / R_total = (1/R1) + (1/R2)

13. Capacitors in Parallel

Total equivalent capacitance for parallel capacitors.

C_total = C1 + C2 + ... + Cn

14. Energy Stored in a Capacitor

Electrostatic energy stored in a charged capacitor.

E = 0.5 × C × V²

15. Energy Stored in an Inductor

Magnetic field energy stored in an inductor.

E = 0.5 × L × I²
